# Product definition

## The product in one sentence

stt-gpui is a desktop push-to-talk dictation app. Hold a global shortcut, speak, and the text streams into the focused app on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Who it is for

The target user is anyone who wants local speech-to-text on the desktop. They dictate into any app they already use, such as a code editor or a chat window. They refuse to send their voice to a cloud service, so the audio never leaves their machine.

Typical users are developers who dictate prompts and commit messages, writers who draft by voice, and people who type less because of strain or injury.

## Language support

French and English are the minimum. Both languages must stream word by word and reach quality the user can dictate with every day. Broader multilingual support is a bonus, not a requirement. PR-3 compares the candidate engines and records the French and English verdict for each.

## What it is not

The app is not a cloud service. It has no account and no audio upload. It is not a transcription tool for existing audio files. It types where you work, while you speak.
