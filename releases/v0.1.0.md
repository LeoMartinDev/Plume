# Plume 0.1.0

The first public version of Plume: local voice dictation for your desktop.

Hold **Ctrl + Space**, speak, and release to insert text into the app you're using. Choose a model, download it once, and keep dictating offline. Your audio and transcription history stay on your computer.

## Features

- Desktop dictation with a customizable shortcut and Esc to cancel.
- Four local models: Nemotron 3.5 Compact, Whisper Base, Whisper Small and Whisper Large-v3-Turbo.
- Automatic language detection, plus French and English selection.
- Automatic insertion, clipboard paste or simulated typing.
- Local transcription history with configurable retention.
- Light, dark and system appearance.
- Plume application icons and a menu bar / system tray entry.
- Updates from stable GitHub Releases, with download verification and user-controlled installation from **Settings → Updates**.

## Downloads

Choose the archive matching your computer:

| Platform | Archive suffix | Launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS Apple Silicon | `aarch64-apple-darwin.tar.gz` | `Plume.app` |
| Windows x64 | `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.zip` | `plume.exe` |
| Linux x64 | `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz` | `plume` |

Extract the entire archive and keep its included files together. On macOS, you can move **Plume.app** to Applications. Speech models are downloaded separately from **Settings → Model**. Each archive includes a SHA-256 checksum file.

Future updates can be downloaded in the app, then installed with **Restart and install**. Plume keeps a copy of the previous app for recovery. Updates require a writable installation folder; source builds and protected folders can use the GitHub download link instead.

## First-version limitations

- Archives are not publisher-signed or notarized; the operating system may ask you to confirm opening the app.
- Linux builds target Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, require X11 and a system tray, and do not yet support Wayland.
- On macOS, grant microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring permissions when requested.

Report problems and suggestions at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/LeoMartinDev/Plume/issues), with your operating system and selected model.
