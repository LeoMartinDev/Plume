# Plume 0.1.4

## First-launch setup

- Add a dedicated onboarding window with three steps: model, shortcuts, and permissions with an optional dictation test.
- Choose between Whisper Base, Nemotron 3.5 Compact, and Whisper Large-v3-Turbo, with lab logos, download sizes, speed and accuracy indicators. The full model catalogue remains available in Settings.
- Download and load the selected model and speech detector before configuring or testing dictation shortcuts.
- Configure push-to-talk and hands-free shortcuts in separate cards. Group microphone and Accessibility permissions with their actions and show the specific permission blocking setup.
- Test transcription inside onboarding without inserting text into another app, changing the clipboard, or adding a history entry. Temporary test audio is removed, including after an interrupted session.
- Save setup progress between launches. Finish closes onboarding directly from the permissions/test step and keeps Plume available in the menu bar or system tray.

## Dictation feedback

- Replace the completion checkmark with a brief contraction and fade of the dictation bubble.
- Give silent takes a small shake and fade, respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Show an explicit unavailable state when the selected model needs to be prepared again.

## Upgrading

Existing installations go through setup once, preserving their selected model, shortcuts and history. Already installed models can be loaded without downloading them again.

On macOS, microphone and Accessibility permissions are required. The development and release bundles use local ad-hoc signatures; replacing a build may require granting permissions again for the current app.
