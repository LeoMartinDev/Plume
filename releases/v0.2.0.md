# Plume 0.2.0

## A new look

- Redesign the onboarding and settings windows to match the website: its colors, the Geist and Instrument Serif typefaces, and rounded pill controls.
- Show onboarding steps in a floating bar, highlight the chosen model card, and let you try dictation on a small stage before finishing.
- Show your push-to-talk shortcut in the settings sidebar; click it to change it.
- Choose the theme from visual previews, and switch the interface between English and French.

## Dictation bubble

- Redesign the bubble: a live waveform while listening, dots while transcribing, a check when the text is inserted, and a clear error state you can dismiss.
- Follow the app's light or dark appearance.
- Go straight to "No speech detected" after a silent recording.

## Text output

- When nothing can receive text, keep it on the clipboard and say so instead of reporting it as inserted.
- Add a Text destination setting to copy every dictation instead of inserting it.

## Reliability

- Esc cancels while a shortcut with extra modifiers is held, instead of opening Task Manager on Windows.
- Recover shortcuts whose release was missed on Windows, X11 and macOS.
- Retry saving recordings when an antivirus or indexer briefly locks the file on Windows.
