# Plume 0.2.1

Plume is local push-to-talk dictation: hold a shortcut, speak, and the text is inserted where you type. Speech recognition runs on your computer.

## Highlights

- Choose a speech model during onboarding (Nemotron 3.5 Compact or Whisper) and try dictation before finishing.
- Hold-to-talk and hands-free shortcuts, with Esc to cancel.
- A dictation bubble with a live waveform, transcription progress and clear errors.
- Insert text into the focused field or copy every dictation to the clipboard; when nothing can receive text, it stays on the clipboard.
- History of recent dictations with retry from the saved audio.
- Light and dark themes, and an English or French interface.

## Updates

- Updates install from Settings → Updates. Installing an update on Windows now works.
