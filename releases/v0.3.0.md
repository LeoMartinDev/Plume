# Plume 0.3.0

## New

- Parakeet TDT v3 from NVIDIA is available as a speech model in Settings → Models. It covers 25 European languages, including French and English.

## Improvements

- On macOS, onboarding now has a dedicated Permissions step before the test. It explains why Plume needs the microphone and Accessibility, and you continue once both are allowed.
- On Windows, the settings window has a subtle border.
