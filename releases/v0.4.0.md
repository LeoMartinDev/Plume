# Plume 0.4.0

## Improvements

- With a Bluetooth headset, the start of a dictation is no longer cut off. Plume waits until the microphone really captures audio before showing Listening, and shows "Opening mic…" in the meantime.
- On macOS, Plume appears in the Dock and the app switcher while its settings are open, so the window no longer gets lost behind other apps, for example after the microphone permission prompt.

## Fixes

- On macOS, dictation now inserts text into Chromium and Electron apps such as Claude, Slack and VS Code.
- On macOS, pasting the transcription no longer types a "v" in some apps instead of the text.
